This paper examines the ways to apply the American Customer Satisfaction Index (ACSI) in Indian industries by considering the peculiarities of the Indian market environment. With the help of a mixed methods research design, the report considers satisfaction indices of customers based on surveys and analysis, covering the sectors of banking, telecommunications, retail trade, and healthcare in India. This paper provides suggestions to implement a tailored satisfaction index for the Indian business environment, along with highlighting the pain areas of respective sectors.
